HonorElevate for Dental Practices

By Connor MacIvor·Published May 16, 2026·7 min read
› Quick Answer

HonorElevate for dental: new patient vs existing routing, insurance intake at first call, recall scheduling for hygiene visits, HIPAA-compliant configuration, integrations with Dentrix, Open Dental, and Eaglesoft. Dental no-show rates start high (20-30%) and drop dramatically with the extended reminder sequence.

Dental-specific qualifying

  • "Are you a new patient or existing?"
  • "What brings you in today?" (emergency vs routine)
  • "Do you have dental insurance?"
  • "When did you last see a dentist?"

Emergency vs routine triage

Emergency = active pain, broken tooth, swelling, infection signs. Same-day appointment if possible. Routine = cleaning, checkup, cosmetic.

HIPAA compliance

Patient data flows through HIPAA-compliant infrastructure. Voice agent does not capture PHI beyond what is necessary for booking. Recording disclosure included in voice agent intro. BAA available with Stripe and underlying platform.

Recall scheduling

6-month recall workflows fire automatically. SMS reminder 30 days, 7 days, day-before. No-show rates drop from typical 20-25% to under 6% with full reminder sequence.

Insurance integration

HonorElevate captures insurance carrier at intake. Does not handle claim filing (Dentrix etc do this). Information flows to practice management software for the staff to handle.

Pricing patterns

  • New patient exam + cleaning: $99-$299 (often discounted as new patient special)
  • Routine hygiene: $120-$200
  • Restorative: $200-$2,500 per procedure
  • Crowns / cosmetic: $1,000-$3,500
  • Implants: $3,000-$5,500 per tooth

Integration partners

Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Curve Dental. Bookings push to practice management for the front desk team.
