How I Turned 9 AI Experts Into a Video, a Blog, and a 7-Platform Campaign Before Lunch

This morning I ran a content engine that took 9 of the top artificial intelligence commentators, pulled the sharpest claim from each, fact-checked every number against the live web, wrote a 20-minute video script, published a 3,200-word blog post, and generated a full 7-platform social campaign. One operator. One morning. This is the exact system, step by step, and why it matters for your business.

TL;DR

  • Gather: 9 expert sources pulled into one research workspace.
  • Extract: the single sharpest claim, number, and contrarian angle from each.
  • Verify: every wild number fact-checked against the live web before anything publishes. This is the step almost everyone skips.
  • Script: a 20-minute teleprompter script, in voice, at a 7th-grade reading level.
  • Publish: a 3,200-word blog post, schema-marked, cross-linked, live.
  • Multiply: a 7-platform social campaign, each post tuned to the platform.
  • Human time: one morning. The machine does the lifting. The human directs and approves.

Most businesses treat content like a chore they lose to every week. A blog they never write. A newsletter that went quiet. Social accounts that post once a month and apologize for the gap. The problem is not effort. The problem is the lack of a system. So let me show you a real one, running, on a real morning, with real output you can click and check.

Today nine of the sharpest voices in artificial intelligence dropped breakdowns. I ran them through a content engine and walked out with a finished campaign before lunch. Here is every step.

› Step 1 · Gather
Nine experts, one workspace

I pulled the day's commentary from nine top artificial intelligence creators into a single research workspace. Not bookmarks. A workspace the machine can read and answer questions against.

For your business: your raw material already exists. Customer questions, job notes, reviews, a single recorded call. The engine starts by pulling your real material into one place it can work from.
› Step 2 · Extract
Pull the sharpest signal, drop the noise

From each source the engine pulled three things: the single sharpest claim, any hard number, and the one angle the mainstream was missing. Then it ranked them. Vague takes got cut. Concrete and contrarian rose to the top.

For your business: the difference between content that converts and content that bores is selection. The engine surfaces the few points worth saying and discards the filler that buries them.
› Step 3 · Verify
Fact-check before a single word ships

This is the step almost everyone skips, and it is the one that protects your name. Before anything published, the engine checked every wild number against the live web. It caught real errors. One source had cross-contaminated two stories and put the wrong technology in a chief executive's mouth. That got corrected. Another claim was softened to match what the primary source actually said.

For your business: artificial intelligence is confidently wrong about a quarter of the time. A content engine without a verification step is a brand-damage machine. The verify step is what makes this safe to put your logo on.
› Step 4 · Script
A 20-minute script, in voice, at a 7th-grade level

The verified points became a teleprompter-ready video script. Written in my voice, at a 7th-grade reading level so a cold viewer never bounces on a big word, with the sources credited so the authority transfers. Zero filler, because filler kills watch-through.

For your business: the engine learns your voice once, then writes in it every time. Your content sounds like you, not like a robot, and it reads at the level your customers actually scan at.
› Step 5 · Publish
A 3,200-word blog post, live and search-ready

The same material became a long-form blog post, marked up with structured data so search engines and artificial intelligence answer engines can read it, cross-linked to related posts, and pushed live. Here is the finished post, on my personal brand, published this morning:

A Machine Built A Smarter Machine Today, And It Did It Without Us › the full written breakdown.

For your business: one idea becomes a search asset that works for years. The engine handles the structured data and cross-linking that most business blogs never bother with, which is exactly why most business blogs get no traffic.
› Step 6 · Multiply
One idea, seven platforms, before lunch

The same content fanned out into a full social campaign, each piece tuned to its platform. A long Facebook post that asks a question. A 30-hashtag Instagram caption. A scroll-stopping TikTok. A business-angle LinkedIn post. A search-optimized Pinterest pin. A six-tweet thread. An email newsletter snippet with subject and preview.

For your business: this is where most owners lose. They make one thing, post it once, and stop. The engine turns every single piece of content into a week of presence across every channel your customers actually use.
