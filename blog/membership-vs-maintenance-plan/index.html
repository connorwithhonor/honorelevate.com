Membership vs Maintenance Plan: When to Layer Each

By Connor MacIvor·Published May 16, 2026·7 min read
› Quick Answer

Maintenance plans cover the operational basics (annual tune-ups, repair discounts). Memberships layer on top with premium benefits (priority dispatch, broader service coverage, exclusive content, concierge access). Plans serve everyone. Memberships serve the segment willing to pay $49-$199/month for elevated service. Most service businesses run plans only. Mature businesses with strong brand can add a membership tier above.

TL;DR

  • Maintenance plan: $24-$30/month. Basic. Covers tune-ups + repair discount.
  • Membership: $49-$199/month. Premium. Bundled benefits, priority access.
  • Both can coexist: plans as entry tier, memberships as upgrade.
  • Adoption rate: 15-25% of plan members upgrade to membership when offered.
  • Math: $79/mo membership × 50 members = $47K annual MRR addition to a typical plan-only operation.

Maintenance plans are the foundation. Memberships are the upgrade tier for customers who want more. This post is the framework for layering both.

Who buys memberships

Roughly 15-25% of plan members upgrade to membership when offered. The segment that buys:

  • Higher-income homeowners
  • Older homeowners (value priority and convenience)
  • Multi-property owners (family coverage applies)
  • Past customers with positive history (trust extends to premium tier)
  • Customers who experienced an emergency without priority access (now want it)

When NOT to add a membership tier

  • If you cannot operationally deliver priority dispatch (creates customer complaints)
  • If you do not have member-only content to deliver
  • If your business is purely reactive (no plan model in place yet)
  • If your customer base is price-sensitive (premium tier will not adopt)

The math for adding a membership tier

Mid-volume HVAC business with 200 active plan members at $24/month ($57.6K annual MRR).

Adding a $79/month membership tier with 20% adoption (40 members):

  • Additional MRR: 40 × $79 = $3,160/month
  • Annual addition: $37,920
  • Plus higher-margin repairs from membership customers (typically 15-20% more per ticket due to deeper relationship)

Total annual revenue lift: $40K-$60K from a single tier added on top of existing plans.
