HonorElevate for Med Spa and Aesthetics

By Connor MacIvor·Published May 16, 2026·7 min read
› Quick Answer

HonorElevate for med spa: treatment-specific qualifying, package booking (memberships for repeat services), before/after photo workflows, social proof emphasis in messaging, compliance for injectable services with medical oversight requirements. Heavy emphasis on warm tone and aesthetic branding.

Med spa-specific qualifying

  • "What service are you interested in? Botox, filler, laser, microneedling, body contouring?"
  • "Is this your first visit or have you been treated before?"
  • "What outcome are you hoping for?"
  • "Any medical conditions or medications we should know about?" (basic intake)

Package booking

Many med spa services sell in packages (5 laser sessions, 3 microneedling, annual maintenance). HonorElevate handles package pricing, session tracking, and renewal workflows.

Before/after photo workflows

Optional patient consent for photos. Captured at first visit and subsequent visits. Tied to the patient record for treatment tracking.

Compliance for injectables

Botox and filler require medical oversight in most states. Lead routing matches qualified providers only. Initial consultation with NP, PA, or physician depending on state. Compliance language included in voice agent intro.

Tone and branding

Med spa voice and chat tone is warmer than service-business default. AI uses words like "gorgeous," "stunning," appropriate to brand. Conversion lift from tone alignment is meaningful.

Pricing patterns

  • Botox: $300-$700 per area
  • Filler: $600-$1,200 per syringe
  • Laser hair removal package: $800-$2,500
  • Microneedling package: $1,200-$2,400
  • Body contouring: $1,500-$8,000
