Inside the HonorElevate Dashboard: A Full Walkthrough

By Connor MacIvor·Published May 16, 2026·9 min read
› Quick Answer

The HonorElevate dashboard has 7 main panels: 5-number summary, pipeline detail, source attribution, tech performance, review monitoring, workflow status, and customer search. Each serves a specific operational or strategic purpose. This post walks through every panel with how to use it.

TL;DR

  • Panel 1: 5-Number Summary. Top of the page. Strategic view.
  • Panel 2: Pipeline Detail. Every open deal by stage.
  • Panel 3: Source Attribution. Revenue and CPBJ by channel.
  • Panel 4: Per-Tech Performance. Bookings, revenue, satisfaction by tech.
  • Panel 5: Review Monitoring. New reviews, average, response rate.
  • Panel 6: Workflow Status. Active automations, success rates.
  • Panel 7: Customer Search. Find any customer instantly.

The dashboard is the operational nerve center of the HonorElevate platform. This post is a panel-by-panel walkthrough.

Panel 1: 5-Number Summary

The top of every dashboard view. Same 5 numbers as the Monday brief: calls handled, pipeline value, appointments booked, closed-won revenue, reviews captured.

Interactivity

Click any number to drill into the underlying detail. Click "Calls handled: 147" and see every call with timestamp, source, outcome, and recording (if applicable).

Panel 2: Pipeline Detail

Every open deal organized by stage. Drag-and-drop to update stage manually. Click any deal to open the full contact record.

Stuck deal flagging

Deals beyond SLA show with red indicators. Quotes 72+ hours old. Booked appointments without confirmation. Owner intervenes accordingly.

Panel 3: Source Attribution

Revenue, booked jobs, and CPBJ by source over the last 30/90/365 days. Sortable, filterable. Discussed in detail in Lead Source Attribution.

Panel 4: Per-Tech Performance

Each tech's bookings, completed jobs, average ticket, customer satisfaction, drive time, and revenue contribution. Compare techs. Identify training opportunities.

Privacy note

Tech-level data visible to owner and dispatcher roles. Techs see their own data only. Avoids unproductive comparison among the team.

Panel 5: Review Monitoring

New reviews this week, trailing average, response rate, sentiment trend. Click any review to draft a response (AI provides draft) and post.

Panel 6: Workflow Status

Which workflows fired this week. Success rates (delivered, opened, responded). Errors flagged for investigation.

Health monitoring

If MCTB stops firing or review requests fail to send, this panel surfaces the problem before it costs revenue.

Panel 7: Customer Search

Type any customer name, phone, address, or email. Full history surfaces in real time. Useful before phone calls (know context before dialing) and during reviews (find past customers quickly).

›
The discipline: the dashboard rewards regular but light engagement. 5-10 minutes daily. Not a "spend an hour staring at metrics" tool. Glance, drill where needed, return to running the business.

Dashboard customization

Owners can rearrange panels, add custom widgets, or hide panels not relevant to their operation. Most owners stay with the default after onboarding because the layout is industry-tuned.

Mobile dashboard

Full dashboard available on mobile with the same panels in a stacked layout. The 5-number summary fits any screen. Drill-down works on phone. Useful for the owner who wants to glance during the workday.
