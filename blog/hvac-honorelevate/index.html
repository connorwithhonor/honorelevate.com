HonorElevate for HVAC Businesses

By Connor MacIvor·Published May 16, 2026·8 min read
› Quick Answer

HonorElevate for HVAC: AI voice agent trained on emergency dispatch protocols, system age intake at every call, replacement candidate flagging at 12+ year systems, ServiceTitan integration, seasonal promo funnels for spring/fall, and the 7:48 PM emergency call walkthrough already in our voice agent guide. The most common deploy across the platform's client base.

HVAC-specific qualifying questions

The AI voice agent asks:

  • "Is the system not blowing at all, blowing but not cold, or making a noise?"
  • "How old is the system?"
  • "What brand?"
  • "Are kids or elderly in the home?" (urgency trigger)
  • "What is the current indoor temperature?" (emergency trigger if 85+ in summer or under 60 in winter)

Emergency vs scheduled triage

Emergency = no cooling in summer + kids/elderly + over 85F indoor. Routes to immediate dispatch. Standard service = anything else. Routes to next-available slot.

Replacement candidate flagging

If system age > 12 years AND any repair quote > $500, lead is auto-tagged "Replacement Candidate." Owner gets notification. Replacement consultation offer fires automatically.

Pricing logic

  • Service call fee: $89 (applied to repair)
  • Standard repairs: $300-$1,500 range
  • Major repairs (compressor, coil): $1,500-$4,500
  • System replacement: $8,500-$18,000 depending on tier

ServiceTitan integration

Bookings push to ServiceTitan dispatch board. Tech assignments fire. Job completion syncs back. Covered in Why ServiceTitan / Jobber Users Still Need a CRM Layer.

Seasonal workflows

  • Spring tune-up funnel: February-May. Covered in The Seasonal Promo Funnel.
  • Fall heater check: September-November. Same pattern, different copy.
  • Summer emergency surge prep: June-August. Capacity planning workflows fire.

Maintenance plan structure

Standard $24/month tune-up plan. Premium $49/month with priority dispatch + IAQ check. Elite $99/month with quarterly visits + family coverage. Plan tiers detailed in Membership Tier Pricing Strategy.

The deploy timeline

5-7 days for standard HVAC deploy. Voice agent training takes the longest (3-5 days). ServiceTitan integration: 1-2 days.
