2. Your email marketing platform

Mailchimp's free tier evaporates the second you grow. Standard sits at $20-$135 a month depending on contacts. ActiveCampaign starts at $49 and climbs past $200 fast. Constant Contact runs $35-$110. The migration order that actually works

You do not migrate everything in one weekend. The smart move is to phase it so each module starts paying for the next before you turn the old one off.

  1. Phase 1 (Days 1-7): Phone + Missed Call Text Back. The fastest ROI move in the entire stack. The day this goes live, you stop losing leads to voicemail. Most owners recover the platform cost inside the first week.
  2. Phase 2 (Days 7-14): CRM and pipelines. Migrate contacts from your old CRM. Set up the pipeline stages that match how your business actually flows. Now every new lead from Phase 1 has a home.
  3. Phase 3 (Days 14-30): Review engine. Wire the post-job review request. Within 30 days you will see review velocity 3-5x what your manual process produced.
  4. Phase 4 (Days 30-45): Email and SMS marketing. Migrate your past-customer list. Build the reactivation sequence. Print money from contacts you already paid to acquire.
  5. Phase 5 (Days 45-60): Funnels, forms, and web chat. Replace your existing landing pages and embed the chat widget. Your top of funnel finally feeds the bottom.
  6. Phase 6 (Days 60+): Cancel the old tools. One subscription at a time. By day 90 you are running on one platform with one bill and one operator's phone number for support.

What HonorElevate does not replace

Be honest about what stays. HonorElevate handles the front of funnel: calls, leads, follow-up, reviews, marketing automation, reporting. It is not a job-management platform. If you run a heavy field-service operation, keep the following:

  • ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro: Field-service dispatch, technician routing, parts inventory, invoicing for completed jobs.
  • QuickBooks or Xero: Bookkeeping, payroll, tax prep.
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft 365: Internal email, docs, video calls.

HonorElevate hands clean qualified leads to those tools via direct API, Zapier, or Make. You keep what works. You seal the leaks around it.
