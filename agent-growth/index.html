The fit test

Most agents should not buy this.

If you close 4 deals a year and want a prettier website, this is overkill. If one missed handoff costs more than the monthly system, keep reading.

You are likely ready when at least one is true:
  • You invest at least $1,500 monthly in portals, leads, ads or growth software
  • You closed at least 12 transaction sides in the last 12 months
  • You operate a team with at least 3 active agents and one accountable system owner
Do not apply if:
  • You want guaranteed leads or closings
  • You refuse to use the CRM
  • Nobody is responsible for response
  • You cannot support a 90-day test

FAQ

Before you apply.

Is this an IDX website?

No. Phase 1 is a complete capture, response, routing, nurture, appointment and reporting system. Live IDX is a contingent Phase 2 module after all required approvals.

Do I have to cancel Zillow or another portal?

No. Keep profitable channels. We compare them against the same pipeline and test a controlled portion of spend.

Are leads or closings guaranteed?

No. HonorElevate guarantees delivery and testing of the agreed operating components, not consumer behavior or licensed-business outcomes.

Can HonorElevate replace my transaction platform?

No. HonorElevate tracks accountability and milestones. The authoritative transaction file stays in the broker-approved system.

Does AI talk to every lead without review?

No. AI assists intake and approved workflows. Licensed people and the responsible broker retain supervision and decision-making.

What forces Team pricing?

Multiple pipelines, more than 3 users, multi-agent round-robin, multi-location routing, a migration above 5,000 contacts or a custom integration.

What happens if IDX approval is delayed or denied?

Phase 1 continues as contracted. Phase 2 is not promised and is quoted only after the approval path is confirmed.
