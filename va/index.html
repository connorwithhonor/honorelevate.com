All the possibilities

How deep do you want to go?

Same spine in all three. The difference is how much of the work stays with your people and how much the machine takes off them. Your answers in the intake below tell me which one actually fits, and I will say so even if it is the smaller one.

Tier One

The Front Door

Prove the talk converts before you automate around it. The right call if you have never run one of these and want evidence first.

  • Branded registration and thank-you pages
  • Live webinar wired to capture every attendee
  • Reminder sequence so people actually show
  • Compliance copy audit on every word
  • Attendance and drop-off reporting
  • No on-demand replay engine
  • Follow-up stays manual

Fits if: you have no proven talk yet and want to test before you invest in the machine.

Where most land
Tier Two

The Machine

The webinar becomes a always-on acquisition engine. This is the version I described as the only one I put my name on.

  • Everything in Tier One
  • On-demand replay. Next session starts in 15 minutes, not Thursday at 6
  • Five-minute speed-to-lead, automated
  • 42-day behavioral follow-up across text, email and voice
  • Two-way texting with carrier registration done properly
  • Full pipeline mapped to behavior, not to stage names
  • 24-month ad version archive, automatic
  • Weekly reporting a human can read

Fits if: you have a talk that works, or a story that has closed a veteran, and the throughput to handle what comes back.

Tier Three

The Overflow

For when the constraint stops being leads and starts being human hours. Only worth it once Tier Two is producing more than your team can answer.

  • Everything in Tier Two
  • AI voice agent answering overflow and booking around the clock
  • IRRRL reactivation against your existing borrower database
  • Per-officer routing, claim windows and scorecards
  • Multi-state disclosure variants that swap by geography
  • Co-marketing sponsor pages, if and only if the arrangement is papered

Fits if: leads are already outrunning your loan officers, or you have a dormant borrower list worth waking up.

What I promise, and the line I do not cross

Anyone who promises you loan volume is lying to you. Lead count rides on your spend. Approvals ride on your underwriting. I own the machine between those two, and I make it run.

What I promise

  • A fully built, tested, end-to-end system
  • Opt-in language that meets the MAP Rule
  • Five-minute speed-to-lead, automated
  • Every ad version archived for 24 months
  • Weekly reporting you can actually read

What I never promise

  • A specific number of raw leads
  • A specific loan approval rate
  • Anything that rides on your ad spend
  • Anything your underwriters control, not me
  • A rate, a term, or a government endorsement

The ceiling
