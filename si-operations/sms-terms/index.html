Program and eligibility

HonorElevate, operated by Connor MacIvor, is preparing Si Operations for internal business notifications to its verified owner. Si Operations is the program name; this notice does not assert a separate legal entity or approved carrier registration.

Eligible alerts concern inbound inquiries, missed calls, appointment changes and operational system issues in the owner’s own business accounts. No customer marketing or general public enrollment is included.

Program number: +1 (661) 299-7543. Support: connor@honorelevate.com.

Consent, frequency and charges

Enrollment must be limited to the authenticated owner and a verified mobile number, with a separate affirmative consent record. A telephone number supplied for another purpose is not SMS consent. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency varies with account activity. Message and data rates may apply under the recipient’s mobile plan. Participation does not change that mobile plan.

Stopping messages and getting help

After activation, reply STOP to opt out of Si Operations texts. The service must suppress further program texts and may send one confirmation of the opt-out. Reply HELP for the sender’s identity and support contact. Fresh affirmative owner enrollment is required to resume after an opt-out.

STOP, HELP, suppression and re-enrollment behavior must be verified before messaging begins. During setup, contact connor@honorelevate.com for assistance.

Delivery and privacy

Delivery depends on the mobile carrier and network. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Use the business CRM and other appropriate channels for time-sensitive or critical matters.
