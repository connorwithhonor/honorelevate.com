Si Operations

Program privacy notice.

Published October 14, 2026 · Program setup pending

This public page provides program information. Online enrollment and automated Si Operations messaging are not active.

Scope and operator

This notice covers the Si Operations owner-only notification program of HonorElevate, operated by Connor MacIvor. It supplements the HonorElevate website privacy policy.

The program is intended only for the verified owner’s own business activity. It is not a marketing list or a customer messaging program.

Information and purposes

The current enrollment information page has no data-submission form, tracking scripts or consent storage. Visiting it does not establish SMS consent. Website hosting may process ordinary request information needed to deliver and protect the site.

Before activation, secure owner enrollment will need to record the owner’s name, verified mobile number, affirmative consent choice, timestamp, disclosure version and source. Those records will be used to administer the program, answer support requests and honor opt-outs.

Operational messages will refer the owner to the authenticated CRM. Private lead details must remain in that CRM rather than on these public pages.

Mobile information and consent

We do not sell mobile information or disclose it to affiliates or third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in records and consent are not shared with third parties for their own use. Service providers may process the minimum information necessary to deliver the messaging service, provide support, maintain security and honor opt-outs, solely on our behalf. These limited service functions do not permit marketing use of mobile information or consent.

Enrollment records will be limited to information needed for program administration and applicable recordkeeping duties. Access must be restricted to authorized administration and service delivery. Information no longer needed will be deleted or de-identified; a minimal suppression record may remain to prevent further messages after an opt-out.

Choices and support

Participation is optional. Once messaging is activated, STOP must end program messages, and HELP must identify the program and provide support. These keyword behaviors are still pending activation and testing. During setup, use the support email for questions or requests.

For access, correction, deletion, privacy questions or an opt-out problem, contact connor@honorelevate.com. Necessary records may be retained to honor a suppression request or meet a legal obligation.

Material changes to this notice will be reflected on this page. A changed messaging purpose requires a new consent decision before use.

Si Operations is an internal program of HonorElevate, operated by Connor MacIvor in Santa Clarita, California.
